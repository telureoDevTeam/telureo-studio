# Telureo Studio

The app tutors go live with on [Telureo](https://telureo.com): your camera with a background and touch-up, your slides and your sound, in one window. It sends your lecture straight to YouTube in HD.

This page holds the installers only. Get Telureo Studio from your Telureo dashboard: open **Lectures**, then **Stream settings** on your lecture.

- Windows 10 or 11
- Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer)

Questions: support@telureo.com
